Larkspur Downtown Crash Report 2024

Prepared by the Larkspur Road Safety Board, published January 15, 2025.

Larkspur's downtown core covers less than two percent of the city's land area, but it accounted for 31 percent of all pedestrian and cyclist injuries recorded by police in 2024. The core saw 412 people injured while walking or cycling, including 9 who were killed. Police data show that the great majority of these crashes involved a private passenger car turning at an intersection or leaving a curbside parking space.

The board notes that the downtown concentration is not new. In each of the last five years the core has produced between 28 and 33 percent of the city's walking and cycling injuries, even as the rest of the city saw modest declines. Intersections with the heaviest turning traffic, such as Mercer and Fourth, recorded more than 40 injuries each.

Board chair Alicia Moreno said the figures reflect how many people share narrow streets with a high volume of cars. "Downtown is where the most people are on foot and also where the most cars are trying to get through," she said. "The risk follows the overlap."
