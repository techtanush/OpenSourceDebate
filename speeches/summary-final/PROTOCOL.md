# Summary / Final Focus Protocol

Used for: the last speeches of a round (summary, final rebuttal, final focus, or the last rebuttal in policy/LD). The job is to **collapse** to your best arguments, **weigh**, and give the judge clear reasons to vote.

Read first: `core/integrity.md`.

## Inputs

- The round flow (what was said, what was dropped), or the transcripts/files if you have them.
- Your own constructive and rebuttal speeches (what you already extended).
- The time limit and wpm.

## Process

1. **List every argument still alive** on both sides, and mark: won, lost, contested, dropped.
2. **Choose what to collapse to.** Pick 1-2 arguments you are clearly winning that carry the most weight. Drop the rest explicitly ("We kick X") if you won't extend them.
3. **Extend with a reason**, not just a name: "Extend [Card]: it says [key point], which wins because [reason], and they never answered it."
4. **Point out their drops**, accurately. Only say "dropped" if they truly didn't answer; check the flow.
5. **Answer their best arguments** briefly and with a reason (don't re-run the whole line-by-line).
6. **Weigh.** Compare impacts on at least two of:
   - Probability: how likely is it?
   - Magnitude: how big and how many people?
   - Timeframe: how soon?
   - Reversibility: can it be undone?
   State why *your* impact wins on each, using evidence from the round.
7. **Voting issues:** 2-3 sentences the judge can write down as the reasons for the decision.

## Structure

```
Opening claim   "This round comes down to X."
Collapse        the one or two arguments you are going for
Their best args short answers
Weighing        comparison on probability / magnitude / timeframe / reversibility
Voting issues   1, 2, 3
```

## Rules

- **No new evidence** in final speeches unless your format allows it; use only what was introduced earlier.
- **No new arguments** late in the round that the other side can't answer.
- **Be accurate about the round.** Don't claim a drop that didn't happen or quote a card differently from how it was read.
- **Clarity over coverage.** A judge who can follow your story will usually vote for you.

## Quality checks

- [ ] You went for a small number of arguments and explained why.
- [ ] Every extension has a reason, not just a card name.
- [ ] Weighing compares your impact to theirs directly.
- [ ] Drops claimed are accurate.
- [ ] The speech fits the time (`speech_timer.py`).
