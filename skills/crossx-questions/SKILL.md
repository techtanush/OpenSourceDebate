# crossx-questions

First read `AGENTS.md` and `core/integrity.md` from the repo root. Never fabricate or paraphrase inside a quote, and never treat a snippet as the source text.

## Read these files

- `speeches/crossx/PROTOCOL.md`
- `core/integrity.md`

## Playbook

The detailed procedure and examples: `speeches/crossx/PROTOCOL.md; core/refutation-guide.md (hole checklist)`

## Steps

1. Read their whole case and list arguments, cards, and cites.
2. Find openings in each card (scope, date, source, their own wording, contradictions).
3. Write closed-ended, one-idea questions tied to specific cards, then a top-five list.
4. Quote their evidence exactly; flag anything you could only see in a snippet.

## Finish with

A short "Before you read these" list: UNVERIFIED quotes, biased or weak sources, missing credentials or dates, and highlighting an opponent could attack.
